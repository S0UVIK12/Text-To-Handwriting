How to convert text to handwriting

  1. Enter your text. Type or paste it onto the paper above.
  2. Pick a style. Choose from 30+ handwriting fonts, set the ink color, font size and spacing, or upload your own handwriting font.
  3. Generate. Click Generate Image. Longer text is split across multiple pages automatically.
  4. Download. Save each page as an image, or download all pages together as a PDF.

Features

  • 30+ handwriting fonts, including Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and Korean options
  • Upload your own handwriting as a TTF or OTF font
  • Blue, black and red ink, adjustable font size, word spacing and letter spacing
  • Rich text editing: headings, bold, italic, underline, lists, alignment and a different font, size, color or highlight for any selection
  • Add images to the page: resize, drag, center or wrap text around them
  • Page layout controls: A4, A5, Letter or Legal paper, ruled, grid, dotted or blank templates, line height and top, left, right and bottom page gaps

  • Multi-page output with one-click PDF download and adjustable resolution
  • Light and dark mode, and it works on phones and tablets

Guide: add your own handwriting

  1. Sign up for Calligraphr
    • Go to Calligraphr and create a free account.
  2. Download a font template
    • Open “Templates”, pick your alphabet, and choose Basic (letters only) or Expanded (numbers, punctuation and more). Calligraphr generates a PDF template.
  3. Print and fill it in
    • Print the template and write each character in its box with a black felt-tip or gel pen.
    • Write neatly and consistently for the best result.
  4. Scan the template
    • Scan at 300 dpi or higher and save it as a PNG or JPEG.
  5. Upload it to Calligraphr
    • Go to “My Fonts” → “Upload Template” and add your scan.
    • Calligraphr builds the characters from your handwriting. Tweak any that look off.
  6. Build and download the font
    • Preview the font, then click “Build Font” and download the TTF or OTF file.
  7. Upload the font to Textor
    • In the Handwriting Options section above, use Upload your handwriting font and select the file you downloaded.
  8. Test and fine-tune
    • Type a few sentences and adjust the size, color and spacing until it looks right.

Frequently asked questions

What is Textor and how does it work?

Textor is a free tool that converts digital text into realistic handwriting. Enter your text, choose a handwriting style, adjust the font size, ink color and spacing, and Textor generates handwritten pages that you can save, share or print. See the guide to adding your own handwriting.

Can I add my own handwriting to Textor?

Yes. Create a custom font from your handwriting using a service like Calligraphr, download it as a TTF or OTF file, and upload it in the Handwriting Options section.

What formats can I export my handwritten text in?

You can download each generated page as an image, or combine all generated pages into a single PDF.

Can I use Textor to convert multiple pages of text?

Yes. Longer text is split across multiple pages automatically, and you can download all the pages together as a PDF.

Is the handwritten text customizable?

Yes. You can change the handwriting font, font size, ink color, word and letter spacing, paper lines and margins, page effects and output resolution.
